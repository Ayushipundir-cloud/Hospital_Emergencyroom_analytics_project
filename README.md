🏥 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor and analyze Emergency Room (ER) operations in real time — tracking patient flow, wait times, satisfaction scores, and department referrals to support data-driven healthcare decisions.

📸 Dashboard Preview
 - [view Dashboard](https://github.com/Ayushipundir-cloud/Hospital_Emergencyroom_analytics_project/blob/main/MIS%20Recording.gif)


📌 Overview
This dashboard provides hospital administrators and ER managers with a centralized view of key operational metrics. By visualizing patient data across time, demographics, and departments, it enables faster decision-making and helps improve patient care quality.

🔍 Dashboard Features
🧍 Patient Volume & Flow

Total patient count with period-over-period comparison (2024 vs 2023)
Monthly patient trend with slicer-based filtering (Jan – Sep)

⏱️ Wait Time Analysis

Average wait time in minutes across the selected period
Trend line visualization for identifying peak hours and bottlenecks

⭐ Patient Satisfaction

Star rating and numeric satisfaction score
Historical trend to track improvement or decline over time

📋 Admission Status

Split between Admitted and Non-Admitted patients
Percentage breakdown for quick operational assessment

🕐 Attendance Status

On-Time vs. Delayed patient attendance (donut chart)
Identifies scheduling and capacity issues at a glance

👥 Demographics Breakdown

By Gender: Male vs. Female patient distribution
By Age Group: Patient volume across 0–09 to 70–79 age bands

🏥 Department Referral Analysis

Patients referred to General Practice, Cardiology, Gastroenterology, Renal, and more
Bar chart format for quick department-level comparison

💡 Key Insights

50% admission rate indicates a balanced ER load — useful for capacity planning
35.88-minute average wait time can be benchmarked against industry standards (target: <30 min)
38% delayed attendance highlights a significant scheduling or triage bottleneck worth investigating
Ages 60–69 have the highest ER visits (74 patients), suggesting elderly care is a critical focus area
General Practice receives the most referrals, indicating potential for pre-ER triaging 

🚀 How to Use

Open the .pbix file in Power BI Desktop
Load your data source — connect to the provided CSV or replace with your own hospital dataset
Use the slicers (Year toggle: 2024/2023, Month filter: Jan–Sep) to explore the data interactively
Publish to Power BI Service for sharing with stakeholders (optional)


📈 Use Cases

Hospital management reporting and weekly ER reviews
Identifying peak hours and staffing gaps
Tracking patient satisfaction trends over time
Department-level referral pattern analysis
Supporting accreditation and compliance reporting
